OUR LITTLE UNIVERSE ♡

1. Extract the whole ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Click the animated envelope, then scroll and explore.
4. Tap photos to enlarge them, open the six notes, and enjoy the final surprise.
5. Optional: turn on soft chimes with the sound button.

CUSTOMIZE
- Edit the messages in index.html.
- Replace images in assets/, keeping the filenames.
- Edit the theme colors at the top of style.css.
- Upload the whole folder to Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages to share a link.

No trackers or third-party JavaScript libraries. Google Fonts requires internet for the custom fonts, but fallback fonts work offline.
